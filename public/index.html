How to download images and videos from a Civit.ai profile

Paste a Civit.ai username or a full profile URL such as https://civitai.com/user/someone and press Load. The browser pulls that creator's gallery and lays every post out as a grid, so you can see an entire profile at once instead of scrolling it page by page. Files come straight from Civit.ai's CDN.

What you can do with the results

Download the images
Every post appears as a thumbnail you can open full size in the lightbox first. Downloads pull the original file at full resolution, not the resized grid preview.
Download the videos
Videos and animated posts are handled alongside images and play inline in the lightbox, so you can check a clip before taking it. Filter to just videos with the type selector.
Select the ones you want
Tick items individually, or shift-click to take a whole run at once. Select the entire page with one button, and your picks persist while you page deeper into the profile.
Download everything as a ZIP file
Download ZIP bundles the whole selection into one archive built on the server and streamed back, so a large batch is a single download instead of hundreds of clicks. An optional speed limit keeps the fetch polite.
Download everything from a profile
Page through the creator's full history, or jump straight to a page, then select all and ZIP. You can also copy the raw URL list or save it as a text file to feed into your own downloader.

Turn what you found into a character you can chat with

Anything you select can go to {{BRAND_NAME}} instead of your downloads folder. Pick the images, hit Send to character, and they become an AI character with a portrait, a gallery and a personality — one you can talk to. Manage the characters you have made on the Gallery page.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse or download — that works straight away. A Civit.ai API key is only needed to see content your account has access to. To send characters to {{BRAND_NAME}} you use your own key from that platform, created on your own account.
